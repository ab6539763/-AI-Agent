# CNC Spindle Overheat & Bearing Inspection (`SOP-CNC-001`)

Revision: `v2.0` (Latest: `v2.0`)

### Procedure Steps
1. Verify emergency stop readiness and isolate main spindle drive.
2. Allow spindle housing to cool below 45 C before physical inspection.
3. Inspect coolant flow rate and clear clogged nozzles.
4. Measure spindle bearing axial play and replace if > 0.02 mm.
5. Run 5-minute no-load warm-up test and verify temperature < 65 C.

### Safety Precautions
- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) main 480V breaker before opening spindle shroud.
- Wear heat-resistant gloves and safety goggles.
