# High-Frequency Vibration & Rotor Re-balancing (`SOP-VIB-002`)

Revision: `v2.0` (Latest: `v2.0`)

### Procedure Steps
1. Capture FFT vibration spectrum at 1000 RPM and 3000 RPM.
2. Inspect tool holder taper and drawbar clamping force.
3. Check anchor bolts and leveling pads on machine base.
4. Perform dynamic trim balancing and verify RMS velocity < 2.8 mm/s.

### Safety Precautions
- Stand clear of rotating chuck during diagnostic spin test.
