# Hydraulic Pressure Surge & Relief Valve Calibration (`SOP-HYD-003`)

Revision: `v2.0` (Latest: `v2.0`)

### Procedure Steps
1. Depressurize hydraulic accumulator via manual bleed valve.
2. Inspect proportional relief valve for spool sticking or debris.
3. Verify pressure transducer calibration against reference gauge.
4. Test cycle at 50% and 100% nominal pressure.

### Safety Precautions
- Confirm zero residual bar pressure on manifold gauge before loosening fittings.
